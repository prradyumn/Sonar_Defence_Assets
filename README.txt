Sonar Defence — 2D cartoon asset pack

Generated with built-in ImageGen, following the supplied cover art style.
Includes all A1–I2 requested entries, seven individual officer poses, both stars, optional cadet, and additional cut-out pieces.
PNG alpha is preserved for props, effects, characters, and overlay layers. Full scenes, sky/surface, and water gradient are opaque.
Dimensions and transparency are listed in asset_manifest.csv.

Animation sheets contain the requested key poses/stages, not interpolated 36-frame animations. Talking gestures may need registration cleanup for smooth playback.
Parallax strips and caustics are tile candidates; pixel-perfect seamless scrolling is not verified.
Original multi-piece sheets are retained alongside cut-outs. Route-progress art is a combined concept strip; extract or overlay a separate ship in implementation.
Numbers and game text are supplied by code.
